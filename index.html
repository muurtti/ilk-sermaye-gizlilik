Ilk Sermaye — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 8 October 2026

In short: Ilk Sermaye does not collect, store or share any personal data. The game does not connect to the internet.

Data collected

None. The game has no accounts, no login, no ads, no analytics and no in-app purchases. It sends no data to any server.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress (cash, stock, shop name, settings) is stored only on your own device. It never leaves your device and never reaches us. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Notifications

If you turn them on, the game may show local reminder notifications. They are scheduled on the device itself; no data is collected or transmitted for this. You can turn them off at any time in the game settings or in your device settings.

Third parties

The game contains no third-party advertising, analytics or tracking services. The store you download it from (Apple App Store) may keep download and crash statistics under its own privacy policy.

Children

The game collects no personal data from anyone of any age.

Changes

If this policy changes, the current version will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
